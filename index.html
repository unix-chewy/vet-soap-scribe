          Questions, answered

          Is this AI completely private? ▼
          Yes. This app uses WebLLM and WebGPU to download and run the AI model directly inside your browser. No patient data, notes, or generated text is ever sent to a server. Everything happens locally on your device.
          Why did the app download a file when I opened it? ▼
          Because the AI runs locally, your browser must download the model weights once. It is roughly 300MB. Once downloaded, it is cached in your browser, and future visits will load instantly without downloading again.
          Does it work on mobile? ▼
          Yes, it works on most modern Android devices using Chrome, and on iOS devices running iOS 26 or later (which enabled WebGPU). We use a highly optimized, small model to ensure it runs smoothly without crashing your phone's browser.
          Can I edit the generated note? ▼
          Absolutely. The output is meant to be a clinical drafting assistant. You can copy individual sections or the whole note and paste it into your EHR to make any final manual adjustments.
